# Capabilities

## Live supervision

Multiple camera panels, people tracking, identity labels, object overlays, camera status, adjustable layouts and appearance-based search.

## People workspace

Named and unnamed person cards, retained face samples, sighting history, editable profiles and identity continuity across camera views.

## Activity reporting

Sitting / standing and working / on-phone / idle estimates, presence history, daily totals, period summaries and idle episodes.

## Floor-plan monitoring

An editor for walls, rooms, doors and furniture; camera placement and coverage directions; calibrated positions, named zones, movement history and route replay.

## Safety monitoring

Handgun detection integrated into the live camera workspace.

## Camera and branch control

Location-based workspaces, camera assignment, connection status and selectable analytics for each camera.
