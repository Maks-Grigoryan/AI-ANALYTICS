# AI ANALYTICS features

## Live camera workspace

- Multiple annotated camera panels with adjustable layouts and camera ordering.
- People, recognized names, posture, activity labels, scene objects and handgun detections.
- Connection status for each camera and branch-based camera selection.
- Name search and clothing-appearance search within the live workspace.

## People database

- Named and unnamed person cards with separate views and counts.
- Retained face samples, sighting counts and first / last appearance.
- Camera references, branch sightings, profile information and clothing observations.
- Naming, profile editing, sample inspection and duplicate-card merging.
- A shared identity workspace across camera views and branches.

## Activity and time

Posture and activity are separate observations. Sitting / standing can appear alongside working, phone use or idle estimates, while presence history remains available for reporting.

- Daily presence and work / phone / idle totals.
- First and last appearance, multi-day summaries and days present.
- Current and historical idle episodes.
- Camera routes and zone routes, depending on the configured map.

## Floor plans

- An uploaded plan or a drawing with walls, rooms, doors and furniture.
- Camera placement, orientation and coverage directions.
- Camera calibration and named zones.
- Live positions, movement trails and recorded zone visits.
- Day replay with a timeline and person selection.

## Handgun detection

A custom detector adds handgun detections to the live camera view alongside the other analysis outputs.

## Camera and branch management

Branch workspaces group cameras, maps and zones by location. Camera management includes assignment, connection status and selection of pose, object, handgun, face and clothing analysis.

The shared people database connects identities and sightings across these workspaces. Connection monitoring, reconnection and explicit camera-session shutdown are part of operation.
